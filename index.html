About

I design the layer between a language model and a real business process.

I'm an Agentic AI Engineer at Genpact, where I build multi-agent systems that don't just answer questions — they execute. Think ticket resolution, enterprise knowledge retrieval, and workflow automation, coordinated by a central Supervisor Agent I designed and orchestrated with LangGraph.

My work sits at the intersection of LLM orchestration, RAG, and distributed systems — hybrid semantic search over Azure PostgreSQL, async communication over Azure Service Bus, and containerized microservices monitored with Prometheus and Grafana.

Before that, I spent six months inside Genpact's data engineering org building ETL pipelines on Airflow and Databricks, and shipping a fraud-detection model that cut false positives by 15%. I like systems that are measured, not just shipped.
